Segregation of duties rules

  • Maker–checker on changes. The requester and the implementer cannot vote on their own change.
  • Build ≠ deploy. The person who built a release cannot sign its gates or deploy it to production.
  • No self-approval of access. Access requests and certifications need a second person.
  • Freeze windows. During a freeze only emergency changes can be implemented or deployed.
